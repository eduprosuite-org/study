Federal Clerk/Stenographer Exam Study Guide

Passing the US Federal typing test requires understanding the specific scoring rules and grading metrics used by federal examiners. Review our comprehensive guide below.

Scoring & Error Calculation Rules

Federal typing tests calculate Net WPM. The formula used is:

Net WPM = (Total Keystrokes / 5 - Errors) / Test Duration (Minutes)

For example, if you type 2,000 keystrokes (400 gross words) in a 5-minute test and make 5 errors, your Net WPM is: (400 - 5) / 5 = 79 WPM.

  • What Counts as a Full Error: Misspelled words, omitted words, inserted words, and formatting errors (such as forgetting to capitalize).
  • Passing Threshold: A minimum of 40 Net WPM is required for most clerk-stenographer positions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum passing typing speed for federal clerk jobs?

The standard minimum speed is 40 Net WPM on a 5-minute test with 95% accuracy.

How are errors penalized on the federal steno typing exam?

Each error deducts one full word from your total word count before dividing by the time in minutes.

Are there formatting requirements in federal exams?

Yes, capitalization, spacing, and punctuation must match the source text exactly. Deviations count as errors.

How to Improve Your Speed - Step-by-Step

  1. Maintain proper posture by keeping your spine straight and wrists elevated above the home row.
  2. Focus on accuracy first. Keep your speed under control; making errors penalizes your score heavily.
  3. Practice daily for 15-20 minutes with business correspondence datasets.
