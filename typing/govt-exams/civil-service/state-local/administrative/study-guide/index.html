State Administrative Assistant Study Guide

State and local clerical exams test a candidate's keyboarding proficiency. Learn how state agencies grade typing speed and follow our practice tips to succeed.

Exam Formats & Evaluation Criteria

State agencies generally use a 3-minute or 5-minute typing test. The scoring system evaluates:

  • Gross Speed: Total words typed in the time limit.
  • Accuracy: Percentage of correctly typed characters. The minimum acceptable accuracy is 95%.
  • Formatting: Transcribing paragraph breaks and double spacing when required.

Frequently Asked Questions

What typing speed do state administrative assistant jobs require?

Most state clerical jobs require a typing speed between 35 and 45 Gross WPM with a minimum 95% accuracy rate.

How long are state civil service typing tests?

They are typically 3 or 5 minutes long, depending on the municipality.

How to Improve Your Speed - Step-by-Step

  1. Warm up your fingers with light typing exercises for 5 minutes before the exam.
  2. Pace yourself. Do not rush the first paragraph; establish a steady rhythm instead.
  3. Practice touch typing without looking at the keyboard to maintain eye contact with the source text.
