Court Reporter Certification Study Guide

Becoming a certified court reporter requires passing high-speed transcription exams. Learn about the standards and evaluation criteria used by national certification boards.

Stenography & QWERTY Standards

The National Court Reporters Association (NCRA) certifies reporters through several exams:

  • Registered Professional Reporter (RPR): Requires transcribing literary text at 180 WPM, jury charge at 200 WPM, and testimony/Q&A dialog at 225 WPM.
  • Legal Secretary/Typist: QWERTY keyboard tests usually require 60 to 70 WPM on legal correspondence.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast must a court reporter type?

Professional stenotype operators must type up to 225 WPM. Legal typists using QWERTY keyboards require 60 to 70 WPM.

What accuracy rate is required for court reporter certification?

The NCRA RPR certification requires a minimum accuracy rate of 97%.

How to Improve Your Speed - Step-by-Step

  1. Familiarize yourself with legal terminology to avoid pausing on unfamiliar words.
  2. Practice double-space formatting and capitalizing speaker identifiers (Q and A).
  3. Train with audio recordings to build real-time conversational transcription speeds.
