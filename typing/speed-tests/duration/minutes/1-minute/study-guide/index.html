1-Minute Typing Speed Guide

Optimizing speed on a 1-minute typing test requires maximizing your burst speed and minimizing finger transition latency. Read our techniques below to break your speed records.

Burst Speed vs. Stamina

A 1-minute typing test represents a sprint. Pacing is less important than raw finger velocity. To maximize speed, make sure you are touch typing correctly with all ten fingers and keeping your keyboard clean to prevent key travel delays.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good score on a 1-minute typing test?

A score of 40 WPM is average, 60 WPM is professional, and 100+ WPM is elite.

How to Improve Your Speed - Step-by-Step

  1. Perform light finger stretches before typing to improve flexibility.
  2. Maintain visual focus on the word ahead of the one you are currently typing.
  3. Avoid using your wrists to drive finger movements; keep wrists steady.
