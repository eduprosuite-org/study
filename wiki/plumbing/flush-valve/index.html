What is Flush Valve?

A valve located at the bottom of a gravity toilet tank that opens to release water and flush the bowl.

Licensing Exam Relevance

Questions concerning Flush Valve are highly common on PSI and Pearson VUE trade exams. Licensing candidates must understand the code restrictions, standard calculations, or material requirements associated with this term to execute design questions successfully.

When answering scenario questions, make sure to cross-reference the index in your IPC/UPC codebook under the letter F to locate exact section rulings.
