What is a Pipe Hanger?

Curator: John Masterson
Published: 2026-08-16
Fact Checked
AI Overview & Quick Facts
A pipe hanger is a structural support device used to suspend, anchor, or secure horizontal or vertical pipelines. Hangers maintain proper drainage slopes and protect piping networks from structural stress, sagging, and vibration.

Core Concept & Mechanism

Piping networks transport heavy volumes of water. Without solid structural support, horizontal pipe runs sag under their own weight. In gravity-flow drainage systems (DWV), sagging creates low spots that collect grease and solids, leading to blockages. Additionally, sagging strains fittings and causes leaks.

Plumbers select hangers based on orientation and pipe material:

  • Clevis Hangers: Adjustable loops suspended from overhead joists by threaded rods, ideal for horizontal drainage runs.
  • Riser Clamps: Heavy metal plates bolted around vertical pipes. They transfer the weight of vertical stacks directly onto floor structures.
  • J-Hooks & Talon Straps: Quick-install plastic or coated supports used for local wood-frame runs.

Galvanic Corrosion Protection: Copper piping must be isolated from dissimilar metals. Supporting copper pipe with bare carbon steel hangers creates a galvanic reaction that corrodes and punctures the pipe. Plumbers must use copper-plated hangers or plastic-coated isolation inserts.

Code & Technical Regulations

Both codes dictate maximum support spacing intervals horizontally and vertically based on the piping material.

Piping Material IPC Max Horizontal Spacing (Table 308.5) UPC Max Horizontal Spacing (Table 313.3)
PEX Tubing 32 inches (2.67 feet) 32 inches (2.67 feet)
PVC / ABS (Plastic) 4 feet (must support at branch joints) 4 feet (must allow expansion sliding)
Copper & Copper Alloy 12 feet (for pipes 1.5 inches and larger) 10 feet (for all pipe sizes)

Journeyman & Master Exam Prep Insights

Support specifications and installation codes are heavily featured on licensing examinations:

  • Horizontal Spacing Charts: Memorize support distances. For example, PEX is supported every 32 inches horizontally, whereas PVC/ABS is supported every 4 feet. Practice these recall questions in the Journeyman Plumber Exam Prep.
  • Galvanic Reaction rules: Understand how copper pipes must be isolated from steel hangers. On exams, using uninsulated steel straps to support copper lines is an automatic violation. Check these corrosion protection rules in the Master Contractor Exam Prep.
  • Vertical Stack Supports: Vertical cast iron stacks require riser clamps at each floor level. Vertical plastic lines require mid-story guides. Study vertical support systems in General Free Prep or Tradesman & Inspector Prep.

Official Citations

  • ICC IPC Section 308: Rules governing piping supports, materials, and spacing frequencies.
  • IAPMO UPC Section 313.0 & Table 313.3: Detailed charts for horizontal support intervals.
  • MSS SP-58: International manufacturing and selection standards for pipe hangers.
