Residential Plumbing Exam Guide: Code Rules & Practice Tests

Written by John Masterson
Published: 2026-08-18
IRC (Chapters 25-32) Aligned

Residential plumbing specialty licenses focus specifically on single-family dwellings, townhomes, and two-family residences governed by the International Residential Code (IRC). This exam assesses essential residential DWV system designs, water service sizing, and water heater safety.

Core Residential Exam Focus

  • Wet Venting: Sizing horizontal bathroom wet vents serving water closets, lavatories, and bathtubs.
  • Water Supply Sizing: Sizing PEX, copper, and CPVC piping based on IRC fixture unit values and pressure loss curves.
  • Water Heater Compliance: T&P relief valve discharge line requirements, pan drains, and expansion tank requirements on closed systems.

Critical IRC Code Requirements Tested

Residential plumbers must master key table lookup questions from the IRC:

  • Trap Arm Distances (Table P3105.1): Maximum allowable distance between fixture trap and its vent (e.g., 5 feet for 1-1/4" pipe, 6 feet for 1-1/2" pipe at 1/4" slope).
  • Building Drain Sizing: Minimum 3" building drain for homes with two or more water closets.
  • Cleanout Clearances: Minimum 18" clearance in front of cleanouts on 3" and larger piping.
