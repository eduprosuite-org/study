Plumbing Code & Inspector Exam Guide: ICC Certifications

Written by John Masterson
Published: 2026-08-18
ICC P1 & P2 Verified

Certified Plumbing Inspectors play a vital regulatory role in enforcing public health and safety codes. Administered nationwide by the International Code Council (ICC), the Residential Plumbing Inspector (P1) and Commercial Plumbing Inspector (P2) certifications evaluate in-depth knowledge of code enforcement, plan review, and site inspection procedures.

Key Testing Highlights

  • Inspection Checkpoints: Underground/slab rough-in tests, top-out/framing inspections, and final fixture trim inspections.
  • Plan Review: Calculating required minimum plumbing fixtures based on building occupancy classifications in the IBC Chapter 29.
  • Testing Standards: Head pressure testing for DWV lines (10-foot water column or 5 PSI air test) and water supply static pressure testing (typically 50 PSI higher than working pressure).

Inspection Stages Evaluated on the Exam

Inspectors must detect non-compliant workmanship across three primary inspection milestones:

  • Underground & Slab: Bedding materials, protection of pipes passing through foundation walls, sleeve clearances, and proper grade slope.
  • Rough-in / Framing Inspection: Structural joist and stud boring/notching rules, nail plate protection within 1-1/4" of stud face, proper trap arm fall, and cleanout access.
  • Final Inspection: Anti-scald thermostatic mixing valve settings (maximum 120°F), air gap vs. air break fixtures, and vacuum breaker installations on hose bibbs.
