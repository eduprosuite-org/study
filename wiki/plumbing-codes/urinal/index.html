Trade Term Dictionary

What is Urinal?

Curator: John Masterson
Fact-Checked & Updated: 2026-08-16
Fact Checked
AI Overview & Quick Facts
A plumbing fixture designed solely for urination, equipped with a water supply for flushing and a trap, regulated for water efficiency and hygiene.

Core Concept & Mechanism

Urinals are high-use fixtures installed in commercial and public restrooms. They must be designed for rapid use and easy cleaning. Modern urinals must balance water efficiency with proper flushing capacity. The maximum water consumption is regulated by energy codes. Waterless urinals are increasingly common, utilizing a chemical trap seal liquid that is lighter than water, allowing urine to pass through while blocking sewer gases. These fixtures require regular maintenance to replace the sealant liquid.

Installation clearance is critical. Urinals must have a minimum of 15 inches from the center to any side wall or partition. Vents must rise vertically above the flood level rim. Waterless urinals must have a water supply line roughed in to allow future conversion to a flushing type if needed. Backflow prevention is required on flushing type urinals using a vacuum breaker.

Code & Technical Regulations

Navigating plumbing standards requires understanding specific code differences. Here is a comparison of design parameters between the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC):

Regulatory Parameter IPC Regulation UPC Regulation
Maximum Flush Volume 0.5 gallons per flush (gpf) for commercial applications (Section 424.1) 0.5 gpf; 0.125 gpf for high-efficiency urinals (Section 412.1)
Minimum Clearance 15 inches from center to side wall, 30 inches center-to-center (Section 405.3.1) 15 inches from center to side wall, 30 inches center-to-center (Section 402.5)
Waterless Urinal Conversion Requires water supply line roughed in for future conversion (Section 424.2) Requires water supply line roughed in for future conversion (Section 412.1.1)

Journeyman & Master Exam Prep Insights

Understanding how licensing exams test this topic is critical for passing. Below are the key focus areas for licensing candidates:

  • Journeyman Plumber Exam Focus: Remember the 15-inch center-to-wall clearance. Waterless urinals require a water supply line to be roughed in during installation. Test your knowledge with practice exams on the Journeyman Plumber Exam Prep page and utilize the General Free Prep sections.
  • Master Plumber & Contractor Focus: Learn how to size drainage lines for battery installations of urinals, and calculate the DFU load on the branch (typically 2 DFUs per urinal). Review complex system layouts and administrative oversight on the Master Contractor Exam Prep page.
  • Tradesman & Inspector Focus: Check that the vacuum breaker is installed at least 6 inches above the flood level rim, and verify that the sealant liquid is added to waterless urinals. Prepare for field layout questions and regulatory compliance scenarios with the resources on the Tradesman & Inspector Exam Prep.
