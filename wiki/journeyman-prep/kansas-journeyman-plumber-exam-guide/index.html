Kansas Journeyman Plumber Exam Guide: ICC & Prometric Prep

Written by John Masterson
Published: 2026-08-18
ICC / Prometric Aligned

In the State of Kansas, trade licensing is administered at the city and county level (such as Sedgwick County/Wichita, Johnson County, Topeka, and Kansas City). Most Kansas jurisdictions contract with ICC (International Code Council) or Prometric to provide standard Journeyman Plumber exams based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Kansas Exam Fast Facts

  • Local Jurisdiction Model: Passing an ICC or Prometric exam is typically recognized across reciprocal Kansas municipalities.
  • Testing Standard: Standard exam comprises 70 questions with a 3-hour time limit based on the adopted edition of the IPC and IFGC.
  • Passing Requirement: A scaled score of 70% or 75% depending on local city/county building codes.

Core Tested Competencies in Kansas

Kansas candidates must demonstrate swift code reference capability across:

  • Venting Configurations: Wet venting, combination waste and vent systems, and terminal freeze protection.
  • Water Heater Relief Valves: Discharge pipe slope, termination clearances, and temperature/pressure safety ratings.
  • Fixture Unit Allocations: Calculating total water supply fixture units (WSFU) and drainage fixture units (DFU) for residential and light commercial structures.
