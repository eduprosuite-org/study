Journeyman Plumber Job Description: Duties, Skills & Responsibilities

Published: August 18, 2026 • Author: John Masterson • 7 min read

A Journeyman Plumber is responsible for installing, assembling, maintaining, and testing plumbing systems in residential, commercial, municipal, and industrial facilities. Operating without supervision, journeymen ensure all piping systems adhere strictly to the International Plumbing Code (IPC), Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and local jurisdictional amendments.

Core Daily Duties & Responsibilities

Whether working for a mechanical contractor, plumbing service firm, or municipal utility, a journeyman plumber performs the following primary functions:

► DWV Rough-Ins & Sizing

Measure, cut, join, and support cast iron, PVC, and ABS drain-waste-vent piping. Calculate correct hydraulic slopes (1/8", 1/4", or 1/2" per foot) and install wet vents, circuit vents, and relief stacks to code.

► Potable Water & Gas Piping

Install Type L/K copper lines via soldering or ProPress, run cross-linked polyethylene (PEX-A/PEX-B) manifolds, assemble black iron/CSST gas distribution lines, and set backflow preventers (RPZ valves).

► Pressure Testing & Commissioning

Perform mandatory rough-in hydrostatic head tests (10-foot water column) and 5 PSI / 15-minute air pressure tests on drainage and gas systems prior to city plumbing inspector sign-offs.

► Blueprint Interpretation & Isometric Drafting

Read architectural, structural, and mechanical drawings to avoid trade collisions (HVAC ducts, electrical conduits) and sketch isometric plumbing schematics for field layouts.

Technical Skills & Qualifications

  • Licensure: Valid state Journeyman Plumber License in good standing.
  • Safety Knowledge: OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 certification; trench excavation shoring and confined-space entry compliance.
  • Tool Proficiency: Operation of threading machines (Rigid 300/535), pressing tools (Milwaukee Force Logic / Ridgid RP), fusion welders, and sewer video inspection rigs.
  • Physical Stamina: Ability to lift up to 75 lbs, bend, crawl in crawlspaces/attics, and work in varying weather conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary daily duties of a journeyman plumber?

Daily duties include interpreting architectural blueprints, installing and roughing in DWV piping, assembling potable water and gas systems, performing hydrostatic pressure tests, setting finish fixtures, and guiding apprentice plumbers on active jobs.

What tools must a journeyman plumber own and operate?

Journeymen routinely use pipe cutters, ProPress tools, threading machines, PEX expanders, torches, drain cameras, hydrostatic testing pumps, and diagnostic equipment.
