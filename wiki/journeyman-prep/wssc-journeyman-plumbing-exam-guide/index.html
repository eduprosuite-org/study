WSSC Journeyman Plumbing Exam Guide: WSSC Code & Testing

Written by John Masterson
Published: 2026-08-18
WSSC Water Code Aligned

The Washington Suburban Sanitary Commission (WSSC Water) regulates plumbing and fuel gas installations across Montgomery and Prince George's counties in Maryland. Operating as an independent bi-county agency, WSSC administers its own licensing and plumbing code amendments.

Key WSSC Testing Points

  • Jurisdiction: WSSC license is mandatory for all plumbing work within Montgomery and Prince George's County territories (independent of Maryland state licensing).
  • Governing Codes: The International Plumbing Code (IPC) and International Fuel Gas Code (IFGC) combined with the extensive WSSC Plumbing and Fuel Gas Regulations (Code Amendments).
  • Key Emphasis: Cross-connection control, water meter vault setting standards, sewer connection permits, and grease abatement systems.

WSSC Specific Regulations Tested

Examinees must pay special attention to sections where WSSC rules supersede national model codes:

  • Cross-Connection & Backflow: Testable requirements for Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) devices and containment backflow on all fire, commercial, and lawn irrigation services.
  • Site-Utility Connections: Strict sizing and inspection criteria for sewer house connections, cleanout placements at property lines, and water service pipe pressure classes.
  • Grease Abatement Systems: Hydro-mechanical vs. gravity grease interceptor sizing formulas mandated by WSSC environmental protection standards.
