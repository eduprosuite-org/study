Digital Business Card Formats & Technical Architecture

A vCard QR code embeds an RFC 2426 electronic business card data stream directly into the QR code matrix. When scanned by Apple iOS Camera or Android Google Lens, the operating system synthesizes a native contact preview sheet allowing immediate address book integration.

📋 vCard 3.0 Data Structure

BEGIN:VCARD\nVERSION:3.0\nN:Last;First;;;\nFN:First Last\nORG:Company\nTEL;TYPE=CELL:Phone\nEMAIL:Email\nURL:Web\nEND:VCARD

1. Contact Exchange Protocols Comparison

Different contact formats offer different balances of data richness versus scanning speed:

Standard Format Data Richness Offline Compatibility Smartphone Native Support Recommended Use Case
vCard 3.0 (Static) High (Name, Phone, Email, Org, Title, Web, Address) 100% Offline (No Server Needed) Universal (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows) Standard Printed Business Cards & Badges
MeCard (NTT DoCoMo) Basic (Name, Phone, Email only) 100% Offline Partial (Older Asian mobile standards) Ultra-compact low-density matrices
Dynamic Contact URL Unlimited (Social links, bio, videos, PDF portfolio) Requires Active Internet Connection Opens web browser first, then downloads .vcf Executive interactive digital profile hubs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vCard QR code?
A vCard QR code encodes virtual contact file data (RFC 2426 vCard 3.0 standard). When scanned by a smartphone camera, the operating system parses the fields and prompts the user to add the contact directly into their phone address book with a single tap.
Which fields can I include in a vCard QR code?
You can encode First Name, Last Name, Mobile Phone, Work Email, Company Organization, Job Title, Website URL, and Physical Street Address.
Why is my vCard QR code more dense than a simple URL QR code?
Because a vCard contains structured multi-line contact text (150 to 350 characters), the QR generator automatically scales to a higher QR Version matrix (e.g. Version 6 or 7) to store all byte characters.
What is the recommended print size for business card QR codes?
For standard 3.5 x 2 inch business cards scanned at 15 to 25 cm, print the QR code at a minimum size of 2.2 x 2.2 cm (0.87 x 0.87 inches) in 300+ DPI vector SVG format.
