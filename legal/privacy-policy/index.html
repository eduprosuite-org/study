Transparency & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Effective for all EduProSuite sites and utilities.

1. Overview & Client-Side Commitment

EduProSuite operates on a privacy-first, client-side model. All interactive generators (such as QR code rendering, exam practice calculations, and typing tests) execute directly within your browser's local sandbox environment. We do not transmit, log, or store your raw inputs, test results, or generated assets on private backend servers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal anonymous operational data necessary to maintain service reliability, including standard web server logs (browser type, referrers, timestamps) and aggregated Google Search Console analytics. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal identity.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Our interactive simulators may utilize your browser's `localStorage` to save your quiz progress, dark/light theme preferences, or test scores locally on your machine. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services & CDNs

We utilize high-speed content delivery networks (e.g., Cloudflare, cdnjs, Google Fonts) to deliver static assets and fonts securely and efficiently. These providers adhere to strict global data security standards.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our administrative team at support@eduprosuite.org.
