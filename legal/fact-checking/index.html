Fact-Checking & Accuracy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Our strict methodology for verifying calculation formulas, state codes, and practice questions.

1. Commitment to Verification

EduProSuite operates in high-stakes informational areas (YMYL) including real estate math calculations, electrical safety regulations (AS/NZS 3000), local municipal zoning guidelines, and trade licensing. We adhere to an uncompromising fact-checking policy before publishing any formula, cheat sheet, or simulation question.

2. Primary Sources Requirement

All factual statements and formulas must originate from primary, authoritative sources:

  • Official State Real Estate Department bulletins (e.g., California DRE Reference Book).
  • National and Regional Electrical Standards (e.g., AS/NZS 3000:2018 Australia/NZ Wiring Rules).
  • Municipal Unified Development Ordinances (UDO) and County Residential Building Codes.
  • Standardized International Plumbing & Mechanical Codes (UPC / IPC).

3. Formula Verification Protocol

Every mathematical solver and quiz calculation is independently computed using automated test suites to ensure edge-case accuracy (such as prorations across leap years, exact Acre square footage conversions, and compound interest calculations).

4. Transparent Error Reporting

We encourage candidates and licensed educators to submit errata. If an error is confirmed, our editorial board corrects the live simulation within 24 business hours. Email verification inquiries to support@eduprosuite.org.
