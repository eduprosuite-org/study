About EduProSuite

Building high-performance, privacy-respecting educational software and open interactive web tools.

Our Mission

EduProSuite was founded to bridge the gap between complex licensing regulations and accessible, interactive study tools. We build instant client-side practice simulators for trade exams (real estate math, electrical codes, plumbing licenses, zoning checklists) as well as vector QR code generation tools, designed to run 100% locally in the browser with zero bloat and maximum privacy.

E-E-A-T & Quality First

We prioritize Google E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) standards across all our knowledge hubs. Every formula, calculation guide, and municipal checklist is curated using official building codes and verified reference documents.

Open & Transparent Engineering

Our projects are open and publicly hosted on GitHub. We utilize modern semantic HTML5, valid Schema JSON-LD structured data, accessible design patterns, and lightning-fast static hosting.
