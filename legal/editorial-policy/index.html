Editorial & Fact-Checking Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Our commitment to rigorous, trustworthy, and non-commodity educational content.

1. Content Integrity & Sourcing

At EduProSuite, we believe accurate study materials are critical for trade licensing and professional exams. All mathematical formulas, building code citations, and practice question rationale are sourced directly from authoritative references, including official state regulatory handbooks, building code standards (e.g., AS/NZS 3000, California DRE Bulletins, Uniform Plumbing Code, and County Zoning Ordinances).

2. Rigorous Fact-Checking Workflow

Every practice question and calculation simulator undergoes a 3-step verification workflow:

  • Mathematical & Formula Accuracy: Independent verification of step-by-step arithmetic (e.g., Cap Rates, LTV, Acreage conversions).
  • Code & Regulatory Alignment: Cross-checking citations against active municipal and state guidelines.
  • User Feedback Integration: Continuous audit and correction based on candidate field reports and examination updates.

3. Corrections & Updates Policy

Building codes and examination formats evolve. When regulatory revisions occur, our team promptly updates practice questions and adds changelog notes. If you detect any potential discrepancy, please submit an immediate correction request to support@eduprosuite.org.

4. Independence & Objectivity

EduProSuite creates unbiased educational software. Our editorial decisions are entirely independent of commercial sponsorships or external test-prep vendors.
