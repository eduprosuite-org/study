Victoria LEA (LET) Practice Quiz

CABLE SELECTION / VOLTAGE DROP

Question 1: What is the maximum allowable voltage drop for a standard installation operating at 230V single phase?

Correct Answer: B) 5% (11.5V)

Under AS/NZS 3000 Wiring Rules, Clause 3.6.2, the voltage drop between the point of supply and any point in an electrical installation must not exceed 5% of the nominal voltage.
