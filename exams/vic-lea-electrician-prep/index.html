Frequently Asked Questions

What references am I allowed to use in the LET exam?
The Victoria LET assessment is an open-book exam. You are permitted to bring physical copies of AS/NZS 3000 (Wiring Rules), AS/NZS 3008.1.1 (Cable Selection), AS/NZS 3012 (Construction Sites), and the Victorian Electrical Safety Regulations. Note that no loose notes are allowed inside the books.
