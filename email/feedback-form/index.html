Frequently Asked Questions

Why use an Email Feedback QR code instead of an online survey form?
Email feedback QR codes launch the customer's native email client with pre-filled questions, allowing them to send direct confidential feedback to executives without filling out third-party survey forms or exposing public negative reviews.
Where should customer feedback QR codes be placed?
Place feedback QR codes on restaurant receipts, hotel room key cards, checkout counter signs, table tents, and product warranty cards.
